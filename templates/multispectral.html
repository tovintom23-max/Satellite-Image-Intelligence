Spectral Band Selection
Default order: 1 = B04 (Red), 2 = B03 (Green), 3 = B02 (Blue), 4 = B08 (NIR). These are one-based indexes. Change them if your GeoTIFF has another band order.

Use Sentinel-2-compatible reflectance data with suitable projected spatial metadata. Ordinary RGB photographs are not compatible.
